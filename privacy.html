Privacy Policy

Travel Checklist & Log
Last updated: March 15, 2026

Overview

Travel Checklist & Log is an offline-first mobile app available for Android and iOS. The app helps you create travel packing checklists, manage trips, and keep a personal travel log.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data, and we do not require accounts.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the information you enter, such as trip name, destination country, trip date, checklist items, trip status, and notes, locally on your device so the app can function.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Purchases

The app may offer an optional one-time Pro upgrade. Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS and are subject to the applicable platform’s payment terms.

Permissions

The core app does not require special permissions to operate.

Security

Your trip and checklist data remain on your device. If you choose to share a checklist or trip summary using your device’s share feature, the content is shared through the apps you select.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.
